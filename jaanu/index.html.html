You're not just my girlfriend, you're my best friend, my baby, my bubu, my wifey, my suji, my everything. You're my person, Jaanu. And nothing and nobody comes anywhere close to you. Things have been difficult, we've had our arguments, misunderstandings and moments where we've probably hurt each other more than we ever intended to. But despite everything, we're still here. We're still choosing each other, and I hope we continue doing that every single day. I don't know if there's a particular reason we've made it through everything, but I do know one thing: I still see you as the one I want to spend my life with. I still see my future with you, and I still want every little thing in life to have you in it. I also want to say that I'm genuinely sorry baby. I'm sorry for the way I've been behaving, for the way I've spoken to you, for the times I've made you feel unwanted, unloved or unappreciated. I'm sorry that even though I love you with absolutely everything I have, I haven't always been able to make you feel that love. And that's something I really need to work on. You deserve to feel loved, valued, respected and appreciated, not just through my words but through my actions too. I know I have a lot to work on within myself, and I don't want to keep making promises without putting in the effort. I want to become better, not just for us but because you deserve the best version of me. And baby, please never think that I'm losing interest in you or falling out of love with you. I'm not. Not even a little bit. I'm still so deeply, ridiculously in love with you. I still get excited about you, I still miss you every single day, and I still want to tell you every little thing that happens in my life. I think sometimes the distance gets to me more than I know how to express. Long distance is genuinely one of the hardest things, and sometimes I wish I could just be there, hug you, hold you, look at you and tell you everything instead of trying to explain it through a screen. But I know it's not you and me against each other. It's us against the distance, and I want us to remember that. I love everything about you Jaanu. Literally everything. Your pretty eyes, your beautiful face, your smile, your voice, your little gestures, your cute habits, your way of talking, your expressions, your anger, your little tantrums, the way you care, the way you make efforts for us and even the tiniest things you probably don't realise you do. I love all those little things that make you YOU. I love having you in my life, I love being yours, and I love calling you mine. You're the best thing that has ever happened to me, and I hope you never have to question how special you are to me. And I genuinely had the best Pujo this year because I got to spend it with you. I loved every single bit of it. Every moment, every little outing, every time we were together, every memory we made. I wish I could relive those days over and over again. I miss being around you so much. I miss having you close, and I wish we didn't have to say goodbye and go back to counting days until we see each other again. But I'm also so excited for everything that's yet to come. All the meetings, dates, trips, silly adventures, festivals, celebrations and all the ordinary little days that I want to spend with you. I really hope we make it through everything, grow together, understand each other better and become happier versions of ourselves. I don't expect everything to be perfect, and I know we'll have difficult days, but I want us to keep trying, keep communicating and keep loving each other through them. I want to learn how to love you better, how to understand you better and how to make you feel as special as you actually are. I want you in this lifetime and every other lifetime. I want the big things with you, but I also want the smallest, most ordinary things. I want to wake up next to you, annoy you, make you laugh, go on random dates, fight over silly things, make up, travel with you, celebrate birthdays and anniversaries, build a home and experience life together. I want all of it, as long as it's with you. Happy 15 months my Jaanu. Thank you for being my baby, my bubu, my girlfriend, my wife and my favourite person in this entire world. I'm so lucky to have you, and I hope I can make you feel just as lucky to have me. I'm sorry for the things I've done wrong, and I promise I'll keep working on myself and putting in the effort to be better. I don't want to just tell you how much I love you. I want you to feel it, every single day. I love you the most baby. More than words can ever explain. It's you, always you, and only you. In this lifetime and every lifetime after this. Happy anniversary jaanu. Riddhima Roy Paul I love you endlessly. ❤️🧿
